SOLANKICRICKET Privacy Policy

SOLANKICRICKET is a cricket tournament scoring application. The current version stores tournament, team, player and match information locally on the user's device.

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit scoring data to a server. Users may export a local JSON backup and share it themselves.

If future versions add analytics, accounts, advertising or cloud synchronization, this policy must be updated before release.
