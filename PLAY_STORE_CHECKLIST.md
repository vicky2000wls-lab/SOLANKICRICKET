# SOLANKICRICKET — Play Store release checklist

1. Create a Google Play Console developer account using your own Google account.
2. Complete Google's identity/contact verification and pay the required registration fee.
3. In Play Console create an app:
   - App name: SOLANKICRICKET
   - Default language: English (or Hindi)
   - App/Game: App
   - Category: Sports
4. Build a signed Android App Bundle (AAB) from this Android Studio project.
5. Upload the AAB to Closed testing first if Google requires it for your account.
6. Add the required testers and complete the required testing period.
7. Complete Store listing, Data safety, App content and target API declarations.
8. Add screenshots, app icon, feature graphic and privacy-policy URL if requested.
9. Apply for production access and publish.

Important: Google account verification, payment, signing ownership, Play Console publication and legal declarations must be completed by the app owner. They cannot be completed on your behalf from this chat.
